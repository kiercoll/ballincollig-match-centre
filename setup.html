Match Centre

Put it on your phone

The Match Centre has fixtures, results and league tables for every Ballincollig side, from the 1st XV down to the U13s. It updates itself every morning.

It works in any browser, but it is worth spending thirty seconds putting it on your home screen. You get the club crest as an icon, it opens full screen with no address bar, and you never have to go hunting for the link again.

iPhone or iPad Android

1iPhone and iPad

This has to be done in Safari. It will not work from Chrome on an iPhone.

  1. Open the Match Centre in Safari. Tap the link the club sent you, or type ballincollig-rfc.netlify.app into the address bar. Wait for the crest and the fixtures to appear.
  2. Tap the Share button. It is the square with an arrow pointing up out of it, in the bar along the bottom of the screen. If you cannot see that bar, scroll up slightly on the page and it comes back.
  3. Scroll down the panel that slides up. Go past Copy, Add to Reading List and Add Bookmark.
  4. Tap “Add to Home Screen”. It has a small square-with-a-plus beside it.
  5. Tap “Add”, top right. The name will already say Collig. Change it to whatever you like, that is what sits under the icon.

2Android

Steps are for Chrome, which is what most Android phones use. Samsung Internet is slightly different and is covered underneath.

  1. Open the Match Centre in Chrome. Tap the link, or type ballincollig-rfc.netlify.app into the address bar.
  2. Tap the three dots. Top right corner, stacked vertically.
  3. Tap “Add to Home screen”. Some versions of Chrome call this Install app instead. Either is the right one.
  4. Tap “Add” or “Install”. If it offers to place the icon for you, choose Add automatically, or drag it where you want it.

Samsung Internet: tap the three lines at the bottom right, then Add page to, then Home screen.

?If something looks wrong

The icon is a grey screenshot instead of the crest
Press and hold the icon, remove it, close the browser tab for the site, then add it again. Phones cache these icons for a while.
The page is blank
Pull down on the page to reload it. If it is still blank, you may be on a very old browser. Updating the browser fixes it.
Nothing has changed since last week
Check the “Data as of” stamp in the top right of the Match Centre. If that date is more than a day or two old, the automatic update has stopped and it needs looking at. Let Kieran know.
My team is not listed
Only sides entered in Munster Rugby's own competition system appear. If a team is missing, it is missing at source rather than here.

iWhere the information comes from

Everything is read straight from Munster Rugby's domestic competition system, the same source behind the Domestic Fixtures & Results pages. Nothing is typed in by hand, so if a result is wrong or a fixture has moved, it is wrong on Munster Rugby's system too and will correct itself here once they fix it.

One thing you will not find is team sheets. Munster Rugby keeps line-ups behind a club-admin login, so there is no public data on who is starting.
